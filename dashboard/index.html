Model Limitation Notes

Honest assessment of what the model cannot do

Duration excluded by design. Duration is the single strongest raw correlate of subscription but is causally after the decision to subscribe — unknown before calling. Including it would inflate model metrics but make the model useless in practice.

Temporal data — not a random sample. The dataset reflects 2008–2013 Portuguese bank campaigns during the post-crisis period. Macro conditions (low Euribor, high unemployment variation) drove significant subscription behaviour. The model may underperform in different economic climates.

Threshold not tuned. The default 0.5 probability threshold was used. Moving the threshold to ~0.3 would increase recall (fewer missed subscribers) at the cost of lower precision (more wasted calls). This tradeoff should be tuned to campaign economics.

Class imbalance. The 8:1 imbalance was handled via class_weight='balanced'. PR-AUC (0.478 vs 0.113 baseline) confirms the model is genuinely predictive, not just predicting the majority class.
