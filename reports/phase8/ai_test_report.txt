Q05: How did the Random Forest model perform?
Source: fallback
Answer:
FACT: Three models were compared on a held-out test set of 8,235 records:
  M0 Dummy baseline:  F1=0.1175, ROC-AUC=0.5032, PR-AUC=0.1134
  M1 Logistic Regression: F1=0.4038, ROC-AUC=0.7820, PR-AUC=0.4129
  M2 Random Forest: F1=0.4815, ROC-AUC=0.8120, PR-AUC=0.4778 (BEST)
FACT: Random Forest 5-fold CV F1=0.4625+/-0.0142 — consistent with test result.
FACT: PR-AUC of 0.4778 is 4.2x the naive baseline of 0.1127.
INTERPRETATION: The CV-test consistency confirms no overfitting. Random Forest captures non-linear feature interactions (e.g. poutcome × pdays) better than Logistic Regression.

Q08: What is the effect of previous campaign outcome on subscription?
Source: fallback
Answer:
FACT: Previous campaign outcome is the strongest categorical predictor:
  poutcome=success (previously contacted): 65.11% conversion (n=1,373)
  poutcome=failure (previously contacted): 51.41% conversion (n=142)
  poutcome=failure (new/not-prev-contacted): 12.94% (n=4,110)
  poutcome=nonexistent (new client): 8.83% (n=35,547)
INTERPRETATION: Re-targeting clients with a prior success history should be the top campaign priority. Even failure outcomes show elevated conversion when the client was genuinely contacted previously.

Q09: What are the limitations of the predictive model?
Source: fallback
Answer:
FACT: Known limitations of the BankInsight predictive model:
  1. 'duration' excluded — the single strongest raw correlate but is target leakage (unknown before the call is placed). Including it inflates metrics artificially and makes the model useless in a real campaign setting.
  2. Dataset scope: 2008-2013 Portuguese bank campaigns during the post-crisis low-rate environment. The macro feature (euribor) may not generalize to different economic periods or regions.
  3. Default threshold (0.5) not tuned: shifting to ~0.3 would improve recall (catch more subscribers) at the cost of lower precision (more wasted calls).
  4. Class imbalance (8:1): despite class_weight='balanced', the minority class is still under-represented. F1=0.4815 and PR-AUC=0.4778 are honest metrics; accuracy (~90%) would be misleading.
  5. 'balance' column: does NOT exist in this dataset (UCI v2). Only UCI v1 contained account balance.
INTERPRETATION: The model is suitable for ranking call lists by subscription probability, not for making hard yes/no predictions. A human review of high-probability cases is recommended.
